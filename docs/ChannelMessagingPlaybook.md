# Channel Messaging Playbook

This playbook standardizes how Soci starts and advances conversations.

## Core principles

1. First message objective is one thing: earn a reply.
2. Personalization is business-contextual, not fake familiarity.
3. First touch contains no hard pitch, no demo link, no calendar link.
4. Pitch comes only after problem acknowledgement or curiosity signal.
5. Meeting link is last card, not first card.

## Universal first-touch structure

1. Observed context (role/company/problem signal)
2. One qualifying question
3. Short close

Template:

`Hey {first_name} — noticed {specific_business_context}. Quick question: {problem_question}?`

## Channel voice guardrails

### LinkedIn
- Tone: professional, concise, neutral confidence
- Length: 1-2 short sentences
- Avoid: hype words, long intros, attachments in opener

### Instagram
- Tone: conversational DM, simple words
- Length: 1-2 lines
- Avoid: email-like formatting, long paragraphs

### Email
- Tone: clear and plain, no fluff
- Length: 40-90 words
- Avoid: long pitch blocks, too many CTAs

### WhatsApp / Telegram
- Tone: human and direct
- Length: short message chunks
- Avoid: heavy formatting and long strategy text

## Opening message bank (by angle)

## Angle A: Problem discovery

- LinkedIn:  
  `Hey {name} — noticed you help {icp_segment} with {service}. Quick question: are you currently doing outbound to consistently bring new prospects into your pipeline?`
- Instagram:  
  `Hey {name} quick one — are you doing any outbound right now to bring new clients in, or mostly referrals/content?`
- Email:  
  `Hi {name}, I saw {company} works with {icp_segment}. Are you currently running any consistent outbound motion for net-new pipeline, or mainly relying on inbound/referrals?`

## Angle B: Current acquisition mix

- LinkedIn:  
  `Curious, {name} — are most new clients for {company} still coming through referrals, or have you built an outbound channel too?`
- Instagram:  
  `Are you guys mostly getting clients from referrals/content right now, or also doing outbound?`
- Email:  
  `Quick question: is your growth at {company} currently mostly inbound/referrals, or do you also have a repeatable outbound channel?`

## Angle C: Competitive observation

- LinkedIn:  
  `Hey {name} — are you currently reaching out to prospects already buying from competitors, or is acquisition mostly inbound today?`
- Instagram:  
  `Do you ever proactively reach out to people already buying from competitors, or mostly wait for inbound?`
- Email:  
  `Do you currently run any outbound toward accounts already buying similar services, or is most acquisition inbound-led?`

## Response ladders

## If prospect says: "Yes, we do outbound"

1. `Nice — how is it performing for consistency right now?`
2. `Where is the biggest bottleneck: targeting, replies, or follow-up cadence?`
3. If bottleneck is explicit, transition to offer:
   `That bottleneck is exactly what we built SociFusion to solve. Want a quick walkthrough on your exact use case?`

## If prospect says: "No, mostly referrals"

1. `Got it. Is adding a predictable outbound channel something you're trying to improve this quarter?`
2. If yes:
   `Makes sense. We help teams turn ICPs into real conversations with multichannel follow-up. Want me to show what that would look like for {company}?`

## If prospect says: "Not interested"

1. `No problem at all — appreciate the reply.`
2. Optional low-pressure close:
   `If priorities change later, I can share one short playbook on building a consistent outbound channel.`
3. Move to nurture in system.

## If prospect says: "Tell me more"

1. One-line value summary:
   `We help teams identify target accounts, start personalized conversations, and follow up across LinkedIn, email, and messaging channels from one flow.`
2. Ask relevance check:
   `Would you prefer a quick example for your niche, or a 2-minute product overview?`
3. If positive signal, then share asset or meeting CTA.

## Offer transition gates

Only introduce SociFusion when one is true:
- prospect admits a pipeline consistency challenge
- prospect asks for details
- prospect asks how you solve it

Do not introduce product if:
- no reply yet
- first reply is neutral with no context
- user asks to keep setup only

## Link and asset policy

- First touch: no links.
- Mid-conversation: share either sales page or webinar link, not both.
- Meeting link: only after interest/fit acknowledgement.
- Never dump all assets in one message.

## Company mention policy

When user names a company in prompt:
- anchor opener on company situation, not generic pitch
- avoid claims that are not evidence-backed
- if confidence is low, ask one clarifying question

## One-shot vs sequence policy

- One-shot requests: one send only, no waits/follow-ups.
- Sequence requests: include pause-on-reply and staged follow-up.
- Setup-only requests: create plan, do not send or auto-launch.

## Message quality checklist (pre-send)

Before Soci sends:
1. Is this message asking a real question?
2. Is it channel-appropriate tone?
3. Is there any premature pitch/link?
4. Is it under channel length threshold?
5. Does it respect user autonomy mode and setup-only intent?

## Anti-patterns to block

- "We help businesses with AI..." wall-of-text opener
- fake hobby personalization
- early demo/calendar push
- same exact script pasted to LinkedIn and Instagram
- promising outcomes without evidence
